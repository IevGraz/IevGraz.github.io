# About this site

You can find a longer version of how this site came to be [here](/2021/09/12/how_this_site_came_to_be.html). But long story short, I have a passion for learning about my craft - software engineering - mostly through books and courses. The more I read and experience, the more I find myself wanting to share it with my colleagues. So I made this site to put it all in one place:

- The conferences [I attended](/conferences.html)
- The things [I read](/book_reviews.html)
- The things [I watch](/reviews.html)
- And the things I experience and decide to [write](/posts.html ) about.
